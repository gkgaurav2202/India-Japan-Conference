\[ \zeta(k_1,\dots,k_r)\;=\sum_{n_1>n_2>\cdots>n_r\ge 1}\frac{1}{n_1^{k_1}\,n_2^{k_2}\cdots n_r^{k_r}} \]

The multiple zeta value of weight \(k_1+\cdots+k_r\) and depth \(r\), for integers \(k_1\ge 2\) and \(k_2,\dots,k_r\ge 1\).

About the conference

The Indo-Japan Conference brings together mathematicians from India and Japan to share recent results, start new research partnerships, and strengthen the long-standing ties between the two mathematical communities. The theme of the conference is Multiple Zeta Values and related areas.

Examples. \(\zeta(2)=\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}\), \(\;\zeta(2,1)=\zeta(3)\) (Euler), and \(\;\zeta(3,1)=\dfrac{\pi^4}{360}\).
