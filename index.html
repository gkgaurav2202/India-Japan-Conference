About the conference

The Indo-Japan Conference brings together mathematicians from India and Japan to share recent results, start new research partnerships, and strengthen the long-standing ties between the two mathematical communities.

Over three days, invited speakers will present talks on current research, with time set aside for discussion, questions, and informal exchange between senior researchers, early-career mathematicians, and students.
